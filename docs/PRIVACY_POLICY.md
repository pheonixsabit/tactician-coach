# Privacy Policy for Tactician Coach

Last Updated: October 2026

Tactician Coach ("we", "our", or "the Application") is an Overwolf application dedicated to providing post-game performance analysis for Teamfight Tactics (TFT). We are committed to protecting your privacy.

## 1. Information We Collect
- **Riot Games Account Identifiers:** When you search or link your account, we process your public Riot ID (`GameName#TagLine`) and Encrypted Player Universally Unique Identifier (PUUID) via the Riot Games API or Overwolf Game Events Provider.
- **TFT Gameplay Statistics:** Completed match statistics, including placement, unit loadouts, star levels, items, augments, damage dealt, and economy round states.
- **Local Settings:** Application preferences, such as selected region and auto-open settings, stored locally on your device via HTML5 LocalStorage / IndexedDB.

## 2. Information We Do NOT Collect
- We do not collect passwords, email addresses, credit cards, real names, or physical locations.
- We do not sell, rent, or monetize your gameplay data to third-party data brokers.

## 3. Data Storage & Retention
- All gameplay statistics and match records are stored locally on your machine.
- You can wipe your stored match history and preferences at any time by clearing application data in Overwolf or through the Settings menu.

## 4. Compliance with Riot Games Policies
Tactician Coach complies with the Riot Games Third-Party Developer Policies and does not collect or display restricted telemetry, such as augment win rates or average placement deltas.

## 5. Contact & Data Deletion Requests
If you have questions regarding this Privacy Policy or wish to request data removal, please contact the development team at `support@tacticiancoach.app` or open an issue on our GitHub repository.
